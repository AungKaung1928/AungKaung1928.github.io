01

In plain words概要

Teaching a small biped to stay up and get back up, on a laptop CPU, after checking that it is possible at all.

小型二足ロボットに「倒れずに立ち続け、倒れたら起き上がる」ことをノートPCのCPUで学習させます。まず、それがそもそも可能かを確かめてから始めます。

The Microduck is a small open-source walking robot. Its authors publish the physics model, the meshes, and a training setup — which requires a CUDA GPU, because that is how modern robot-learning frameworks run thousands of simulated robots in parallel.

Microduckは小型のオープンソース歩行ロボットです。作者は物理モデル、メッシュ、学習環境を公開していますが、 その学習環境にはCUDA GPUが必要です。最近のrobot learningフレームワークは、GPU上で数千台のシミュレーションロボットを並列に動かす前提だからです。

There is no GPU here and no hardware to buy. So this project runs the same physics model in plain CPU MuJoCo, parallel across processes instead of GPU threads, inside a budget of eight of the machine's fourteen cores, on a laptop that has other work to do.

ここにはGPUもなく、実機を買う予定もありません。そこで本プロジェクトでは、同じ物理モデルを通常のCPU版MuJoCoで動かし、 GPUスレッドの代わりにプロセスで並列化します。使うのは14コア中8コアまで。他の作業も抱えたノートPC上での実行です。

The task is not walking. It is standing still under three seeded shoves per five-second episode and getting back up when one of them wins. Every episode runs its full 250 steps, so a policy that lies down is scored for lying down instead of being cut off early.

タスクは歩行ではありません。5秒間のepisodeごとにseedで決まる3回の押し（push）を受けながら立ち続け、 押しに負けて倒れたら起き上がることです。どのepisodeも必ず250 step最後まで走らせるため、寝転んだpolicyは途中で打ち切られず、寝転んだまま採点されます。

The first two steps train nothing, on purpose. The third trains.

最初の2ステップでは、意図的に何も学習させません。学習は3つ目からです。

What each step is for各ステップの目的

Step 1 is a feasibility gate: is this machine fast enough to train a policy at all, and what is the honest number to budget against? Step 2 is the environment contract: what does the policy see, what does it emit, when does an episode end, and what number does it have to beat? Both were written before any learning code, because a training run that fails is uninterpretable if you cannot rule out the machine and the environment first. Step 3 re-decides the reward on the numbers step 2 produced, then runs PPO against the PD controller. Step 4 randomises the physics and measures what that is worth on held-out models; step 5 exports the policy. Step 3 has run its full 50M steps and step 4 the first half of its own; steps 4 and 5 are measured on step 3's policy at the same halfway point.

Step 1は実現可能性の判定（feasibility gate）です。このマシンでそもそもpolicyを学習できる速度が出るのか、 計画の前提にすべき正直な数値はいくつか、を確かめます。Step 2は環境の契約（environment contract）です。 policyが何を見て何を出力するか、episodeがいつ終わるか、超えるべき数値は何かを定めます。 どちらも学習コードより先に書きました。マシンと環境の問題を先に排除できなければ、学習が失敗しても原因を解釈できないからです。 Step 3ではstep 2で得た数値をもとにrewardを決め直し、PD controllerを相手にPPOを走らせます。 Step 4ではphysicsをランダム化し、held-outモデルでその効果を計測します。Step 5ではpolicyをexportします。 Step 3は50M stepをすべて完走し、step 4は自身の前半を終えています。Step 4と5は、step 3のpolicyの同じ中間時点のものを使って計測しています。

Why "sim-to-sim" is the real target本当の目標が「sim-to-sim」である理由

The robot's authors ship four physics variants of the same robot — including one with a passive backlash joint inserted in series with every actuated joint, which is a much more realistic model of a cheap hobby servo's slop. All four expose the same fourteen actuators, in the same order, under the same names.

ロボットの作者は、同じロボットの物理モデルを4種類公開しています。その中には、駆動関節すべてに受動的なbacklash関節を直列に挟んだモデルもあり、 安価なホビーサーボのガタをはるかに現実的に再現しています。4種類とも、同じ14個のアクチュエータを同じ順番・同じ名前で持っています。

That means a policy trained on one variant can be run on another with no remapping at all. The held-out physics is supplied by the robot's own authors rather than invented here — a far stronger position than randomising some parameters and then testing on the same randomisation, which is the standard and much weaker version of this experiment.

つまり、あるモデルで学習したpolicyを、対応付けを一切変えずに別のモデルで動かせます。held-outのphysicsはここで作ったものではなく、 ロボットの作者自身が提供したものです。いくつかのパラメータをランダム化し、同じランダム化の上でテストするという一般的な（そしてずっと弱い）実験より、 はるかに強い立場で評価できます。

Terms used on this pageこのページの用語

policyThe function mapping what the robot senses to what it commands. This is the thing being learned.ロボットが感知した情報を指令値に変換する関数。学習の対象そのものです。
observationThe vector the policy reads each control step — here 48 numbers: joint positions and velocities, the previous action, the gyro, and gravity in body frame.policyが制御stepごとに読むベクトル。ここでは48個の数値で、関節の位置と速度、直前のaction、ジャイロ、胴体座標系での重力方向です。
action scaleHow far one policy output is allowed to move a joint away from its neutral position. Too large and the robot thrashes.policyの1回の出力で、関節を基準姿勢からどこまで動かせるか。大きすぎるとロボットが暴れます。
PD controllerA fixed control law: command proportional to position error plus a term proportional to how fast that error is changing. No learning.固定の制御則。位置誤差に比例する項と、その誤差の変化速度に比例する項の和を指令します。学習はしません。
env-steps/sSimulation throughput. It is the number that decides whether training takes an hour or a week.シミュレーションのthroughput。学習が1時間で終わるか1週間かかるかを決める数値です。
domain randomisationVarying physics and appearance during training so the policy is not tuned to one exact model of the robot.学習中にphysicsや見た目を変化させ、policyがロボットの特定の1モデルだけに合わせ込まれないようにする手法。
system IDMeasuring the real actuator to find out how it actually behaves, which then sets an honest range to randomise over.実際のアクチュエータを計測して実際の挙動を調べること。その結果が、ランダム化の妥当な範囲を決めます。
sim-to-realThe gap between a policy that works in simulation and one that works on the hardware. The whole point of the exercise.シミュレーションで動くpolicyと、実機で動くpolicyとの差。この取り組み全体の目的です。
PPOProximal Policy Optimization. The policy is nudged toward actions that scored above expectation, with each update clipped so one batch cannot move it far.Proximal Policy Optimization。期待より高く評価されたactionの方向へpolicyを少しずつ寄せます。各更新はclipされ、1つのbatchでpolicyが大きく動かないようにします。
GAEGeneralised advantage estimation: how much better than expected each step went, blended over future steps by a discount γ and a mixing factor λ.Generalised advantage estimation。各stepが期待よりどれだけ良かったかを、割引率γと混合係数λで将来のstepにわたって合成したものです。
running normaliserA running mean and variance of every observation seen, used to put all 48 channels on the same scale before the network sees them. It is saved with the policy.これまでに見たすべてのobservationの移動平均と分散。ネットワークに入る前に48チャネルすべてのスケールを揃えるために使い、policyと一緒に保存します。
truncation vs terminationAn episode that ends because time ran out (truncation) still has a future worth something; one that ends because the task failed (termination) does not. Every episode here is a truncation.時間切れで終わったepisode（truncation）には、その先にもまだ価値があります。タスク失敗で終わったepisode（termination）にはありません。ここではすべてのepisodeがtruncationです。
clip fraction / KLTwo per-update health readings: how many samples hit PPO's clip, and how far the new policy moved from the old one. Both high means the step size is aggressive.更新ごとの2つの健全性指標。PPOのclipに当たったサンプルの割合と、新しいpolicyが古いpolicyからどれだけ動いたか。両方が高ければstep sizeが攻めすぎです。
held-out physicsRobot models the policy is evaluated on but never trained on. Here they come from the robot's own authors.評価には使うが学習には一度も使わないロボットモデル。ここではロボットの作者自身が提供したものです。
ONNXA portable file format for a trained network. The exported graph runs in ONNX Runtime with no PyTorch on the robot.学習済みネットワークの可搬なファイル形式。exportしたグラフはONNX Runtimeで動き、ロボット側にPyTorchは不要です。

The metric that would have got this wrong結論を誤らせるところだった指標

Read by survival, the randomised policy is a clear win: 1.00 against 0.74 on the training model and 0.99 against 0.56 on backlash, and the first version of the evaluation script led with that column. But survival only asks whether the trunk is above 4 cm. The randomised policy is upright for 28% of steps against 47%, its trunk sits at a median of 9 cm, and its return splits 134 from the upright term against the nominal policy's 183. It learned to brace in a low, tilted crouch that never counts as a fall, not to stand. Upright share and trunk height went into the metrics module after that; the rerun reproduced every return to the decimal.

survivalで見れば、ランダム化したpolicyの明らかな勝ちです。学習用モデルで1.00対0.74、backlashで0.99対0.56で、評価スクリプトの最初の版はこの列を先頭に出していました。 しかしsurvivalが問うのは、胴体が4 cmより上にあるかどうかだけです。ランダム化したpolicyが直立していたのはstepの28%（公称は47%）で、胴体の高さの中央値は9 cm、 returnのうちupright項からの分は134（公称のpolicyは183）でした。このpolicyが学んだのは立つことではなく、転倒とは判定されない低く傾いたしゃがみ姿勢で踏ん張ることでした。 その後、直立の割合と胴体の高さを指標モジュールに加えました。再実行では、すべてのreturnが小数点以下まで再現しました。

Backlash: the randomised policy barely notices it (+3.2 return, −0.04 upright), while the nominal one loses 15 and 0.09 and falls more often. That is the robustness randomisation is meant to buy, and it bought it, but around a worse posture. The nominal policy on backlash is still upright more often than the randomised one on the model it trained on. Rollers: the randomised policy collapses, losing 165 of return, with its median trunk 2 mm above the fall line. The nominal policy does better on rollers than on its own model, so for a policy that actually stands this is not a harder physics model at all. A crouch held up by randomised foot friction has nothing to push against on free-rolling contacts. That is a hypothesis, not a measurement.

Backlash：ランダム化したpolicyはほとんど影響を受けません（return +3.2、upright −0.04）。一方、公称のpolicyは15と0.09を失い、倒れる頻度も増えます。 これはランダム化で得ようとしていた頑健性そのもので、実際に得られましたが、より悪い姿勢を軸にしたものでした。backlash上の公称policyでさえ、学習に使ったモデル上のランダム化policyより直立している時間が長いのです。 Rollers：ランダム化したpolicyは崩壊し、returnを165失い、胴体の高さの中央値は転倒ラインのわずか2 mm上でした。公称のpolicyはrollersの方が自分のモデルより良い結果なので、 実際に立てるpolicyにとっては、これはまったく難しい物理モデルではありません。ランダム化した足の摩擦に支えられたしゃがみ姿勢は、自由に転がる接触では踏ん張る先がない、と考えられます。これは仮説であり、計測ではありません。

Net: on this robot, under this reward, at half the schedule and one seed, domain randomisation made the policy worse. That is the question step 4 was built to answer, and it is reported as it came out. What it does not settle: both runs share step 3's step-size problem, milder here (approximate KL rising to 0.32 against 0.76), and neither used the KL stop added afterwards. The likely cause is the reward. Its height term is linear and blind to tilt, so a crouch at 9 cm earns about 1.2 per step every step, against 2.0 for a stand that sometimes ends on the floor near 0.5. Randomised physics makes standing harder to learn and bracing safe under every sample. Testing that needs a new reward version with its own baseline, not a tweak to this one.

結論：このロボット、このreward、予定の半分の学習量、seed 1つという条件では、domain randomisationはpolicyを悪化させました。 これがstep 4で答えるべき問いであり、出た結果をそのまま報告しています。未解決の点もあります。2つの実行はどちらもstep 3のstep sizeの問題を抱えており（ここでは近似KLが0.76に対して0.32まで上昇と軽め）、 後から加えたKL stopはどちらにも使っていません。原因として最も考えられるのはrewardです。height項は線形で傾きを見ないため、9 cmのしゃがみ姿勢は毎step約1.2を稼ぎ続けます。 これに対し、立つ姿勢は2.0を得られるものの、ときどき床に倒れて0.5付近になります。ランダム化したphysicsのもとでは立つことの学習が難しくなり、踏ん張る姿勢はどのサンプルでも安全になります。 これを検証するには、このrewardを微調整するのではなく、独自のbaselineを持つ新しいrewardバージョンが必要です。

Step 5 — one graph, normaliser includedStep 5 — normaliserを含む1つのグラフ

The export design, the two checks that run before any timing, and the one-thread latency table for the chunk-1 policy.

exportの設計、時間計測の前に行う2つのチェック、chunk 1のpolicyの1スレッドlatencyの表。

export_onnx.py writes one ONNX graph from the raw 48-dim observation to the 14-dim mean action, with the normaliser folded in as (obs − mean) / std, clipped. A policy shipped without its normaliser is a different policy, and a controller on the robot should not have to know the normaliser existed.

export_onnx.pyは、生の48次元observationから14次元の平均actionまでを1つのONNXグラフとして出力します。normaliserは(obs − mean) / std（clip付き）として組み込みます。 normaliserなしで出荷したpolicyは別のpolicyです。ロボット側のcontrollerが、normaliserの存在を意識しなくて済むようにすべきです。

Verification comes before timing. 1,000 observations from the policy's own rollouts, not white noise, whose range the normaliser has never seen, go through both paths and the maximum difference must be under 1e−5. test_export.py proves the fold with a random actor: the graph disagrees with the un-normalised actor and agrees with the training-time path, and a huge observation gives the same finite answer both ways, so the clip survived export. Then ONNX Runtime latency on one intra-op thread, batch 1, p50 and p99 over 2,000 calls, which is the shape an on-robot controller runs at 50 Hz.

時間計測より先に検証を行います。normaliserが一度も見たことのない範囲のホワイトノイズではなく、policy自身のrolloutから取った1,000個のobservationを両方の経路に通し、 最大差が1e−5未満であることを求めます。test_export.pyはランダムなactorで組み込みを証明します。グラフは正規化なしのactorとは一致せず、学習時の経路とは一致します。 また極端に大きなobservationを入れても両方で同じ有限の値が返るので、clipがexport後も残っていることがわかります。そのうえで、ONNX Runtimeのlatencyを1 intra-opスレッド・batch 1で2,000回呼び出し、p50とp99を計測します。 これはロボット上のcontrollerが50 Hzで動くときの形です。

python export_onnx.py runs/ppo_v2_chunk1.pt   # -> runs/policy_v2.onnx, runs/onnx_v2.json

The same environment in C++C++で書いた同じ環境

A sibling repository that reproduces this environment to the bit and what it found out about this one.

この環境をビット単位で再現する姉妹リポジトリと、それがこの環境について明らかにしたこと。

A separate project, a threaded C++17 backend, ports env.py and vec_env.py to one process with eight threads and a pybind11 binding, and runs ppo.py on it unchanged. Its first claim is equality, not speed: 6,275 of 6,275 observations across 25 episodes are bit-identical to this environment's, which took matching numpy's summation order, Python's pow and a float32 constant. Its second claim is the one that matters here: alternating the two backends in one run, its threads deliver 1.9 to 2.0× this vector environment at eight workers. Its third is this page's step 3, run again on that backend with the same configuration (except one reward weight, 21× weaker on C++ until a 2026-11-21 fix; retrain pending) and training seed: 50M steps in 3.1 hours instead of 4.4, and a policy that scores 341.9 ± 4.1 of 500 on this environment against 344.8 ± 2.9 for the one trained here, inside the evaluation noise. This step run again on seeds 1 and 2 scores 357.3 and 342.2 with survival 0.999 and 0.895: the 0.73 here is the low draw of three, and across three seeds per backend neither return nor survival differs (339.4 ± 2.3 against 348.1 ± 8.1, survival 0.94 ± 0.06 against 0.87 ± 0.14).

The short version要約

The whole project compressed into one paragraph.

プロジェクト全体を1段落にまとめたもの。

A balance-and-recover task for a 14-servo open-source biped, built to run entirely on a laptop CPU because the upstream training stack requires CUDA. Step 1 is a feasibility gate whose answer arrived four times, 28 749, then 18 400, then 8 000, then 13 300 environment steps per second, and the write-up keeps every revision, including the one that was wrong in the conservative direction. Along the way it found that the walking model's contact mask lets the robot fall through the floor, that the backlash variant renumbers the state vector, and that the robot's own authors disagree about joint friction by 6.7×, which is where the randomisation range comes from. Step 2 defines the environment: a 48-dimensional observation cut down from 61 so every channel is one real hardware could produce, 14 residual actions with a clip that exists because the control range is 26× wider than the tightest joint limit, fixed-length episodes with seeded pushes, and a reward with a measured PD baseline of 108.7 ± 2.9 out of 500. A review found five silent defects that twenty-seven passing tests had not. Step 3 rebuilt the reward on the numbers, dropping two dead penalties, replacing a flat height Gaussian with a ramp, setting the joint-velocity weight from its measured share and rejecting the obvious upright fix because the fallen duck never tilts past 90°; the PD baseline under the new reward is 175.2 ± 3.1. PPO with a running normaliser and a truncation bootstrap then ran its first 25-million-step chunk, and running it added a fifth revision to the throughput figure: about 3 300 environment steps per second with the policy in the loop, a quarter of the plan, because the eight pipe round trips and the network forward pass had never been in the estimate. The first chunk's step size ran away, with a clip fraction of 0.73 and KL spikes above 50. The second chunk resumed with a KL stop and finished the 50M steps at 344.8 ± 2.9 against the PD controller's 175.8, with every seed up on chunk 1's 332.6 (section 13). Step 4 trained a second policy under randomisation over the measured servo range and tested both on two held-out models from upstream. The randomised policy never counted as fallen, but it had learned to brace in a crouch instead of standing. It shrugged off backlash and collapsed on rollers, a model the nominal policy found easier than its own, so on this reward and this schedule randomisation made things worse. The ONNX export, with the normaliser folded in, runs at 0.033 ms p99 on one thread.
