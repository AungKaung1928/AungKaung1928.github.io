Microduck Balance and Push Recovery

microduck-rl

A balance-and-recover policy for a 25 cm, 14-servo biped, trained with PPO in CPU MuJoCo against a PD baseline. Domain randomisation was run, came out worse on held-out physics, and is reported as such.

25 cm・14サーボの二足ロボットに、CPU版MuJoCoでPPOによるバランス維持・復帰policyを学習し、PD baselineと比較。Domain randomisationはheld-out物理で悪化し、そのまま報告しています。

A C++ Environment That Matches Python to the Bit

mujoco-vecenv-cpp

The Microduck environment rewritten in C++17 on threads: 6,275 of 6,275 observations bit-identical to Python first, speed second. Same PPO loop, no difference in the trained policy.

Microduck環境をC++17・スレッド並列で再実装。まず6,275個のobservationがPythonとビット単位で一致することを示し、速度はその後。同じPPOループで、学習結果に差はありません。

One Arm, Three Ways to Learn It

Sub-Millimetre Cube Pose

mujoco-cube-pose-cnn

A 27 k-parameter CNN recovers a cube's position and yaw from one image to 0.59 mm median. Two classical baselines first; the second exposed a radial bias in the first.

27 kパラメータのCNNが画像1枚から立方体の位置とyawを中央値0.59 mmで推定。先に古典的baselineを2つ作り、2つ目が1つ目の半径方向のbiasを発見。

Watching a Robot Fleet

Three simulated robots publish position; Kafka carries it to two readers — one stores every row, one raises an alert when a robot stops. The whole fleet is one YAML file, proven end to end in CI.
